FLOOD COMMAND CENTER
====================

Multi-Agent Flood Risk & Disaster Advisory Console using live weather,
hydrology, and public incident data to generate real-time risk scores,
evacuation zones, response priorities, and emergency SMS recommendations
through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.


OVERVIEW
--------
The system combines live public data providers with a LangGraph multi-agent
pipeline and a FastAPI streaming backend. It is designed for rapid disaster
monitoring, explainable risk assessment, and coordinated emergency response.


CORE CAPABILITIES
-----------------
- Live weather and rainfall monitoring across major Mumbai flood-prone zones.
- Modeled hydrology and river-discharge monitoring.
- Public incident intelligence from live Google News RSS results.
- Telemetry Agent for weather and hydrology analysis.
- Social Listening Agent for incident location and severity extraction.
- Dispatch Synthesizer for risk scoring and response prioritization.
- Interactive Streamlit and Pydeck command-center dashboard.
- Color-coded risk map with animated high-risk zones and dispatch arcs.
- Emergency SMS drafting and simulated broadcast workflow.
- Automatic provider health reporting and deterministic fallback handling.


LIVE DATA SOURCES
-----------------
- Open-Meteo Forecast API:
  Current precipitation, rainfall, and wind data.
- Open-Meteo Flood API:
  Modeled river-discharge data used as a transparent hydrology risk proxy.
- Google News RSS:
  Current public reports matching the configured Mumbai flood query.


TECHNOLOGY STACK
----------------
- Python 3.11+
- FastAPI and Uvicorn
- LangGraph and LangChain
- Streamlit and Pydeck
- Pydantic
- Open-Meteo and Google News RSS integrations
- Pytest


OUTPUTS
-------
- Real-time city and zone risk scores from 1 to 100.
- Risk classifications: Low, Medium, High, and Critical.
- Evacuation zones.
- Prioritized emergency resources.
- Agent handoff and analysis traces.
- Emergency SMS recommendations.
- Live provider status and fallback indicators.


DATA INTEGRITY
--------------
Live provider failures do not crash the application. The affected provider
falls back independently to deterministic local data, and the dashboard
clearly labels the result as LIVE, MIXED, or FALLBACK.

SMS delivery is simulated unless a real messaging provider is configured.
